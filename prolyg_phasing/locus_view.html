PolyG loci from panel.db

Read from the extracted panel only, not from the BAM: the page shows the reads the fit and the phasing use. Dropped reads appear only in the counters. Every sequence is in polyG orientation (the G strand): at a "-" locus the bases are reverse-complemented and reference coordinates fall from left to right.

Columns: the upstream flanking bases on their reference columns, the upstream flank, the tract, the downstream flank, the downstream flanking bases. The upstream side is fixed: every row's tract starts in the same column. The downstream flank and flanking bases follow each row's own tract, so they line up only between rows with tracts of one length. A "." in the panel (no read base) is drawn as a gap. The panel holds no reference bases outside the tract, so the reference row shows coordinates there.
